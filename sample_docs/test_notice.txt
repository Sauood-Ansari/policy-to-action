NOTICE
Department of Technical Education
Summer Internship Scholarship 2025

Applications are invited from eligible students for the Summer Internship Scholarship 2025.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.5
- Applicable for 2nd and 3rd year students only
- Branch: Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics
- No active backlog at the time of application
- Indian nationals only

SCHOLARSHIP AMOUNT:
Stipend of Rs. 15,000 per month for 2 months

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Marksheet of previous semester
2. Bonafide certificate from college
3. Aadhar card (self-attested copy)
4. Bank passbook (first page)
5. Passport size photograph (2 copies)
6. NOC from Head of Department
7. Statement of Purpose (SOP) – maximum 500 words

HOW TO APPLY:
Students must register on the portal: https://scholarships.example.gov.in
Fill the online application form and upload all required documents.

LAST DATE TO APPLY: 30/06/2025
Interview Date: July 15, 2025

For queries, contact: scholarships@example.gov.in
